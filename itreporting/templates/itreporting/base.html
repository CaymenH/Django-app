Sheffield 4 Fun Facts

  • 1. The Worlds oldest football club, Sheffield is home to sheffied F.C. founded in 1857 - officially recogmised by FIFA as the worlds oldest football club.
  • 2. A City built in a Rainforest, Sheffield sits in a natural bowl of seven hills (like Rome) but its most remarkable feature is being the U.K`s greenest city with over 2 million trees.
  • 3. The Steel City Sheffield gave the world stainless steel and was the birthplace of the crucible steel process, at its peak it produced 90% of Britains steel.
  • 4. The Snooker capital of the world, Sheffield has produced more Snooker Champions than any other city and has hosted the world Snooker Championship at the Crucibke Theatre since 1977.
